How entering the game works

After you click Enter Game, Arkade City uses 330 sats to publish a registration marker and keeps another 330 sats in your player wallet as the reusable action-asset carrier.

You only send funds to your own player address. The app handles the shared registry output inside the registration transaction. Moves do not pay the registry.

How actions are tracked

W, D, S, A, bullets, and lives are six immutable Arkade asset groups. Every action burns exactly one matching unit and carries that player's next contiguous sequence number.

When moves or bullets run out, Mint New Action Pack creates a fresh registration. It requires 660 BTC-only sats, keeps 330 sats in the new carrier, and resets your player ID, position, HP, and kills; a future shop will replace this temporary flow.

There is no game server, host, or relay. Browsers validate every burn for inventory continuity, then replay all indexed actions sequentially in global (createdAt, txid) order. Later historical indexer data can change that replay.

Your selected action is previewed immediately, but authoritative position, HP, and kills change only after the indexer supplies its timestamp. The preview remains visible while indexing catches up.
